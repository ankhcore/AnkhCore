Notebook fan configurations (this folder)
-----------------------------------------
The 243 *.xml files here are the community notebook fan configurations of the NoteBook FanControl project (NBFC / NBFC-Revive):
they document, per notebook model, the embedded-controller registers that set the fan speed.
They are shipped UNMODIFIED, as data. AnkhCore's own fan-control engine reads them; no NBFC program is installed or run.

  Project:  https://github.com/hirschmann/nbfc  and the NBFC-Revive fork
